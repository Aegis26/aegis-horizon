---
name: Manual call scope
description: Product decision for call handling after Phase 4
---

Rule: Calls are logged manually as ordinary account activities. Automated calling, recording, transcription, and Twilio/AssemblyAI integration are outside the current product scope.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose to keep Phase 4 focused on email sync, calendar sync, internal collaboration, Slack, and conversation intelligence.

**How to apply:** Preserve the manual Call option in account timelines. Do not reintroduce recording providers, call webhooks, transcript storage, or call-recording UI unless the user starts a later automated-calling phase.
